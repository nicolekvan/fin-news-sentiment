How to label (read once)
Bullish
The text points to a likely positive business or financial impact for the named company: beats, raised guidance, a won contract, an upgrade.
Bearish
The text points to a likely negative impact for that company: misses, cuts, lawsuits, lost deals, downgrades.
Neutral
It is about the company, but nothing in it should move the business either way (scheduling, routine announcements, balanced commentary).
Unclear
It is about the company, but the headline and summary do not give enough to judge, or the signals truly conflict.
Not about it
The company is only named in passing (a list of tickers, a comparison) or the match is wrong.

Judge only the company named at the top of the card, using only the headline and summary shown. The same story can be good for one company and bad for another. Do not look up what the stock did afterwards. The model's answer is hidden on purpose so it cannot influence you.
